# PrimeVul pairwise accuracy by CVE year

430 scored pairs (5 validation-overlap pairs removed), CVE years 2008-2022. Generated by `code/evaluation/cve_year.py` from the 92 held-out runs found (runs below 95% pair coverage skipped).

## Summary

The trend score (the `rho` column below) is a number from -1 to +1 that tells you whether a model does better on older bugs or on newer ones.

- Below 0: better on older bugs. That would hint the model memorized them, since old bugs have been online longer.
- About 0: the bug's age doesn't matter.
- Above 0: better on newer bugs.

What we found: every trend score is close to 0, between -0.17 and +0.09. Nine of the 92 runs lean slightly toward older bugs (p < 0.05), and six of those come from the Opus session, including the Opus 4.6 and Opus 5 iter 3 harnesses (about -0.10). For most of them the lean comes from lower accuracy on 2022 bugs, which are 45% of the pairs, not from a steady drop with age. With 2022 removed, 4 runs are still significant: Opus 4.6 iter 1 and Inkling iter 10 lean toward older bugs, and Opus 5 iter 2 and GLM-5.3 iter 2 lean toward newer ones.

The harness gains don't depend on older bugs: on 2022 pairs alone, Nemotron and Inkling (iter 12) beat their seeds by 8.3 and 9.8 points, close to their overall gains of 8.4 and 11.9.
